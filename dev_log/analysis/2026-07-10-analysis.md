### The one genuine semantic conflict
Our fork added "disable hf_transfer on retry" inside an **inline** retry body.
Upstream had refactored the download to use a prebuilt `$ODYSSEUS_HF_CLI` /
`hf_download_args` abstraction, dropping the inline body. Resolution: ported our
fallback (`HF_HUB_ENABLE_HF_TRANSFER=0` on retry `_attempt > 1`) into upstream's
abstraction — preserving the fix, not discarding it.

### Why we kept our JS (not upstream's)
Our JS carried deliberate local fixes upstream lacks: Windows download/scan/serve
path contract (`bd7106e`), tmux session kill/probe (`_downloadSessionTask`,
`_tmuxCmd`), and HF_HOME env setup in the preview command. Taking pure upstream
would delete working features and break our running build. Verified by test:
keeping ours passes the profile-submodule regression test; taking upstream broke it.
